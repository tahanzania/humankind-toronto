## **About Us**

## **Who We Are**

humanKIND toronto was founded in 2017 by a small group of women who believed kindness should be active — not passive. Founded by Ashifa Champsi \- you could replace it by saying “consists of a small group of women” which we’ve used in the past. 

We are a volunteer-led nonprofit working across the Greater Toronto Area to support women, children, and youth living in shelters.

Many of the individuals we serve are navigating through domestic abuse, homelessness, trauma, and profound isolation. Our role is not to “fix” their journey — but to walk beside them in it.

We partner with six shelters and currently deliver six monthly programs designed to foster healing, empowerment, and personal growth.

Everything we do is grounded in four core values:

* **Compassion**: We lead with heart.

* **Kindness**: We act with intention.

* **Empathy**: We listen first.

* **Community**: We grow together.

We believe real change happens when people come together, consistently, humbly, and wholeheartedly.

## **2019 — Growing With Purpose**

As relationships deepened and trust was built, the work began to expand. What started as one monthly program gradually grew into several. Additional shelters welcomed programming, and the circle of volunteers and supporters widened.

By the end of 2023, humanKIND toronto was delivering multiple programs across shelters in the Greater Toronto Area, creating consistent spaces where women and children could gather, create, reflect, and feel supported. The foundation of the organization was no longer just an idea; it was a growing community.

---

## **2020 \- Creativity Through Crisis**

When the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted in-person programming in 2020, humanKIND toronto faced one of its most defining moments. Rather than pause, the team chose to adapt.

Virtual workshops were introduced, including online art sessions and creative programming designed to continue supporting women and children remotely. This shift not only allowed the organization to maintain its commitment locally, but also expanded its reach across Ontario. Through virtual delivery, humanKIND toronto was able to expand their reach and offer workshops to shelters outside of Toronto, offer workshops to all Ontario locations of Sakeenah Canada, something that had not previously been possible. 

During this same period, community support grew significantly. Individuals and small businesses hosted fundraisers, donated proceeds from yoga classes and baked goods, and created handmade items in support of the mission. Social media became a powerful tool for connection and awareness, leading to invitations to share the organization’s story through live online conversations and participation in a documentary feature.

What could have been a season of pause instead became a season of resilience and expansion.

---

## **2023–Present — A Growing Circle**

In the years that followed, humanKIND toronto continued to grow steadily and intentionally. Today, the organization offers seven monthly programs across six shelters in the Greater Toronto Area, while also leading annual drives that provide essential resources to women, children, and youth in need.

What began in 2017 as one woman’s expression of gratitude has grown into a dedicated network of volunteers, partners, donors, and community members who believe deeply in showing up, consistently and compassionately.

Through every season of growth and challenge, one belief has remained unchanged: the need never stops.

And neither do we.

## **Programs**

## **Our Programs**

At humanKIND toronto, our programs are created to offer more than just activities. They create moments of healing, connection, and joy.

We partner with shelters across the Greater Toronto Area to provide regular on-site and virtual programs for women and children. Each session is thoughtfully designed to foster self-expression, build confidence, and create a sense of community for those navigating difficult circumstances.

Whether through creativity, conversation, or shared experiences, our goal is to create welcoming spaces where participants feel supported, valued, and empowered.

### **Life Skills**

Our life skills workshops are designed to provide practical tools that support confidence, independence, and personal growth. These sessions may focus on everyday strategies, goal setting, wellness habits, and meaningful conversations that encourage participants as they rebuild.

### **Cooking Workshops**

These sessions offer opportunities for relaxation, nourishment, and self-care. Through gentle yoga classes and interactive cooking demonstrations, participants are invited to slow down, learn new skills, and experience moments of calm and care.

### **Art Sessions**

Art provides a powerful outlet for healing and self-expression. Our creative workshops give women and children the opportunity to explore, create, and connect in a supportive environment where the process matters more than the outcome.

### **Shared Meals**

There is something deeply meaningful about gathering around food. Our shared meal programs bring women and children together in community, offering not just nourishment but connection, conversation, and belonging.

### **Why It Matters**

Many of the women and children we support are navigating trauma, uncertainty, and isolation.

These programs offer more than a service — they offer dignity, encouragement, and moments of hope.

## **Sock Drive**

It’s one of the most requested and most overlooked essentials.

For women and children living in shelters, a new pair of socks can mean warmth, comfort, and a small but powerful sense of dignity. Something that is often taken for granted becomes something deeply needed.

At humanKIND toronto, our annual Sock Drive is rooted in a simple idea: no one should have to go without the basics.

Each year, our community comes together to collect **new socks for both children and adults**, helping ensure that individuals of all ages have access to something as simple and essential as clean, comfortable socks.

These small items are delivered with care, reminding every recipient that they are seen, supported, and not forgotten.

### **How You Can Help**

* Donate new socks (children’s and adult sizes)  
* Organize a collection drive in your school, workplace, or community

Project Lunchbox was created to support women and children in shelters with thoughtfully prepared meal kits and essential food items. For many families navigating transition and hardship, access to reliable, nourishing food can make a meaningful difference in their day-to-day lives.

## **Gift of Hope**

Leaving a shelter is a powerful step  but it can also be uncertain.

For many women and children, transitioning out of a shelter means starting over with very little of their own. Gift of Hope was created to support that moment — to ensure that as they move forward, they don’t leave empty-handed.

This initiative provides essential bedding and comfort items that individuals can take with them as they begin their next chapter. Items that are theirs to keep. Items that help turn a new space into something that feels like home.

Each Gift of Hope box includes:

* A duvet  
* A duvet cover  
* A pillow  
* Pillowcases  
* A bath towel  
* A bedsheet

These are more than household items.  
 They are a sense of ownership.  
 A sense of stability.  
 A small but meaningful foundation for a fresh start.

### **Why It Matters**

Starting over is not easy. Having something of your own; something new, something chosen with care  can make that transition feel just a little more supported.

Gift of Hope is about dignity in that moment of change.  
 It’s about sending someone forward with comfort, care, and the reminder that they are not alone.
