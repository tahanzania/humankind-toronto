Make a donation

Give with intention

Every act of kindness creates a ripple.

every gift creates a ripple

When you donate to humanKIND toronto, you are directly supporting women, children, and youth living in shelters across the Greater Toronto Area — individuals who are rebuilding their lives after experiencing abuse, homelessness, and hardship.

Your support helps us

  • Deliver monthly healing-centered programs
  • Provide essential items through our annual drives
  • Create safe, creative spaces for connection and growth
  • Expand our reach to more shelters and communities

No donation is too small. Every contribution matters.
