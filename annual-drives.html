Our partners

Local businesses, giving back.

Our Community Partners have been an essential part of the success we’ve achieved in collecting items to donate to our local shelters. By partnering with local businesses who have housed collection boxes onsite, not only are we able to encourage more community members to contribute to our seasonal drives, but we are also able to afford local businesses more ways to give back.

If you would like to get involved by hosting a collection drive on behalf of humanKIND toronto, or participate in an upcoming collection drive as a local business, we’d love to hear from you.
